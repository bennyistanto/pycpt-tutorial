# Story chapters

The first chapter is [Twelve seasons over Indonesia](indonesia-seasons-2023.qmd).
It embeds figures from the executed companion notebook and includes generated
numeric findings. The second is [Fit & diagnose](fit-diagnose-2023.qmd); the third,
[Issued 1 November 2023: the next six months](monthly-leads-2023.qmd), quotes the tables
written by its executed notebook, as does the fourth,
[Issued 1 November 2023: the next six seasons](seasonal-leads-2023.qmd), the fifth,
[Issued 31 October 2023: the next three dekads](dekad-leads-2023.qmd), the sixth,
[Issued 1 November 2023: the next four weeks](week-leads-2023.qmd), and the seventh,
[Issued 31 October 2023: the next six pentads](pentad-leads-2023.qmd).

Each of those five time scales also has a multi-model ensemble chapter:
[seasonal](seasonal-mme-2023.qmd) and [monthly](monthly-mme-2023.qmd) (seven models,
issued 1 June 2023), and [dekad](dekad-mme-2023.qmd), [weekly](week-mme-2023.qmd) and
[pentad](pentad-mme-2023.qmd) (five SubX models). Every number in them comes from the tables their
executed notebooks write under `docs/data/`.
